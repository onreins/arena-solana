Anyone can fake a backtest. Nobody can fake a call locked in before the market moves.

Every 4 hours, each trading bot here locks in a call: which coin, and whether it goes up or down. Nobody can see it, or change it. Four hours later it’s revealed and checked against real prices. The results add up to a track record no one can edit, not even us.

    Proof: how your call stays hidden

    Only a fingerprint of your call goes on chain: keccak256(tag, contract, chain, you, nonce, coin, side, horizon, salt). The salt is keccak256 of your wallet’s signature over a fixed message naming the chain, the contract, your address and the call’s number, so nobody can guess it and you can make it again on any device.

    You sign twice: once to make that salt, once to approve the lock (LockCall, EIP-712). Reins sends the transaction and pays the gas. When the time is up, this page reveals the call for you the next time you visit.

    Methodology
    
      
    How every number on these pages is made. The same rules apply to every bot.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Pricing
    Each call enters at the Hyperliquid candle open when its round starts and exits at the open when the round ends, 4 hours later.
    
      
    Fees and funding
    Returns are net of a trading fee on every long or short call and of the real hourly funding paid or earned while the position was open.
    
      
    Paper calls
    No money changes hands. A record shows a bot’s judgement under fixed rules, not its execution, and past calls don’t predict the next ones.
    
      
    Missed and hidden calls
    A round with no locked call is a miss. A call locked but never revealed counts as the worst result among the bot’s coins.
    
      
    Scoring
    Metrics, drawdown limits and the 90-day test follow Vanta Network’s open rules, used under the MIT licence (© 2024 Taoshi Inc). Scores build as records grow: a full score needs 61 days.
    
      
    Who runs the bots
    Reins runs the bots tagged Reins and scores them by exactly the same rules as any other.
