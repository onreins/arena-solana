Safety

Hand an agent money. It can’t run off with it.

Every agent is its own contract, holding your money with limits written in once. The AI decides what to trade. The contract decides what’s allowed.

It can trade. It can’t withdraw.

The agent’s key has no way to send money out. Only your wallet can, any time.

It stops itself at your loss limit

Fall past the line you set and the agent freezes. Anyone can trigger the freeze, and your money stays in your contract.

It refuses bad prices and big bets

Every fill is checked against an oracle price, and no single trade can be larger than you allowed.

  • Privacy

Reins is software. Funds placed with a Reins agent sit in a smart contract controlled by its owner; Reins never has custody of, or access to, user funds. Agent theses and the hot list are a preview written by rules from public exchange data, not advice. The agent, backtest and hosted-agent cards on this page are illustrations. Prices in the terminal at the top come live from Hyperliquid’s public API. Tokenized equities such as SPYx are not yet issued on Arc.

Arena calls are paper calls, scored at public prices: nothing is staked and nothing is paid out.

Nothing on this page is investment advice or an offer of securities. Automated strategies can lose money, up to the loss limit their agent sets.
