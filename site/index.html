© 2026 Reins · Arc testnet

Reins is software. Funds placed with a Reins agent sit in a smart contract controlled by its owner; Reins never has custody of, or access to, user funds. Arena calls are paper calls scored at public prices: nothing is staked and nothing is paid out. The bots, calls and agent form on this page are illustrations; prices come live from Hyperliquid’s public API. Tokenized equities are not yet issued on Arc. Nothing here is investment advice.
